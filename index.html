Frequently asked

Questions residents will ask before they sign up.

These are starter answers. Your final operating rules should replace the placeholders before launch.

What days do you pick up trash?

Standard pickup is Tuesday and Thursday. Pickup days may vary by community or service area, and your exact collection window is confirmed at signup.

Where do I leave my trash?

Residents place securely tied household trash outside their unit door during the approved pickup window. Community-specific placement rules may apply.

Do you replace my regular trash company?

No. This service is designed to move resident trash from the doorstep to the property’s existing dumpster, compactor, or designated waste area.

How does the introductory rate work?

The introductory rate is reserved for the first 100 customers who complete signup and payment. Clicking the offer does not use up a spot — only a completed signup does.

You pay $18/month for your first 12 months. After that the plan continues at the standard $28/month rate. You can cancel any time from your account, and nothing is charged after you cancel.

Can a property manager sign up the entire community?

Yes. Property managers can request custom community pricing based on unit count, layout, collection logistics, and service requirements.

What items are not accepted?

Final restrictions should be published before launch. Typical exclusions may include hazardous materials, oversized items, loose liquids, construction debris, and unbagged waste.
